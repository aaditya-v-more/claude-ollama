# Code of conduct

This is a small project, so this is a short document.

Take part as you would anywhere people are trying to get something working:
assume the person on the other end is acting in good faith, say what you mean
plainly, and leave room for someone who knows less about this than you do. A
question that has been asked before is still a question worth answering.

Not welcome, in issues, pull requests, commit messages, or anywhere else this
project happens: harassment of any kind, insults, and personal or political
attacks; comments demeaning to someone for who they are — their gender, sexual
identity or orientation, disability, appearance, body size, race, ethnicity,
age, religion, or nationality; sexual language or imagery; publishing anyone's
private information without their permission; and deliberately wasting other
people's time.

This applies to anyone taking part, and it applies in public spaces where
someone is representing the project.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to **aadityavmore@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and
kept private. Tell me what happened and where; a link is usually enough.

The response is whatever fits: often a word with the person involved, sometimes
editing or removing a comment, and, for anything repeated or deliberate,
blocking the account from the repository. Being the one who reported something
will not cost you anything here.
